# Workflow Pack — Recon

## Objective

Guide early engagement work toward meaningful context gathering before deeper validation and exploitation decisions.

## Checklist

- Identify exposed surface, tech stack clues, identities, trust boundaries, and historical signal.
- Record hypotheses worth validating during enumeration.
- Rank findings by likely value, confidence, and scope relevance.

## Outputs

- Exposed surface inventory.
- Prioritized hypotheses for enumeration.
- Short rationale for where to spend time next.

## Guardrails

- Separate observation from inference.
- Avoid noisy collection that does not improve later decision-making.
- Keep scope and legality visible in every recon step.

## Evidence

- Source records, screenshots, timestamps, and asset inventory artifacts.
- Confidence tags for major recon conclusions.
