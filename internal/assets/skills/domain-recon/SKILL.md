# Domain Pack — Recon

## Objective

Guide reconnaissance toward high-signal target understanding that improves later enumeration and exploitation decisions.

## Checklist

- Build target inventory from approved sources and observable attack surface.
- Collect context on services, identities, tech stack, third parties, and historical exposure.
- Rank hypotheses by expected value and confidence before deeper validation.

## Outputs

- Target inventory with confidence level and source attribution.
- Prioritized recon findings that feed next-step enumeration.
- Notes on unknowns that still need verification.

## Guardrails

- Respect scope and legal boundaries for passive and active collection.
- Separate confirmed observations from inference.
- Do not bury the operator in low-signal noise.

## Evidence

- Source references, timestamps, screenshots, and resolved assets.
- Confidence notes for each important recon conclusion.
