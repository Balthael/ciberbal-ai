# Workflow Pack — Reporting

## Objective

Guide the transition from technical validation to stakeholder-ready reporting that is clear, reproducible, and action-oriented.

## Checklist

- Convert evidence into concise findings with severity, impact, and remediation.
- Tailor the narrative for both technical and leadership audiences without losing accuracy.
- Ensure every finding can be traced back to reproducible proof.

## Outputs

- Finalized finding narratives with severity and remediation.
- Executive summary themes tied to systemic risk.
- Retest-ready notes for future validation.

## Guardrails

- Avoid vague claims or unsupported severity inflation.
- Separate confirmed exploitation from reasonable but unproven extrapolation.
- Keep remediation practical and specific where possible.

## Evidence

- Linked artifacts, reproduction steps, screenshots, and raw proof used to support the narrative.
- Editorial notes explaining any uncertainty or limitation.
