# Domain Pack — WiFi/Wireless

## Objective

Guide wireless assessments toward validated access, segmentation, and environmental risk findings instead of generic radio observations.

## Checklist

- Document target SSIDs, encryption/auth methods, coverage assumptions, and physical constraints.
- Validate authentication weakness, rogue infrastructure exposure, segmentation failures, and client attack paths.
- Track whether success depends on proximity, timing, or user interaction.

## Outputs

- Wireless findings tied to real access or trust-boundary impact.
- Environmental notes explaining feasibility and repeatability.
- Recommended mitigations for auth, segmentation, and monitoring gaps.

## Guardrails

- Keep capture, deauth, and rogue-infra testing within approved rules of engagement.
- Do not imply universal exploitability when the result depends on narrow physical conditions.
- Document lab, office, or site assumptions explicitly.

## Evidence

- Capture metadata, screenshots, auth outcomes, and network placement proof.
- Notes on location, signal conditions, and test timing.
