# Domain Pack — Reporting

## Objective

Guide reporting work toward trustworthy, reproducible, and action-oriented deliverables that serve both technical and stakeholder readers.

## Checklist

- Confirm each finding has reproducible steps, impact explanation, and remediation guidance.
- Separate executive narrative, technical detail, and evidence cleanly.
- Normalize terminology, severity rationale, and remediation expectations across findings.

## Outputs

- Stakeholder-ready findings with severity, impact, reproduction, and remediation.
- Evidence index tying artifacts back to each claim.
- Follow-up notes for retest or verification planning.

## Guardrails

- Do not inflate severity without impact proof.
- Avoid vague remediation language when a concrete control change is known.
- Preserve reader trust by marking uncertainty explicitly.

## Evidence

- Screenshots, logs, commands, requests/responses, and references used in the final narrative.
- Traceability from raw evidence to final finding text.
