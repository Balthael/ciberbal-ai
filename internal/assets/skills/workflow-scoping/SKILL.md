# Workflow Pack — Scoping

## Objective

Guide the engagement toward clear targets, assumptions, constraints, and success criteria before active validation begins.

## Checklist

- Confirm scope boundaries, target set, accounts, restrictions, timing, and communication paths.
- Record assumptions, dependencies, and explicit out-of-scope items.
- Resolve ambiguity early enough to avoid invalid evidence later.

## Outputs

- Scoping summary with approved targets and constraints.
- Rules of engagement and escalation path for unexpected findings.
- Risk notes covering ambiguity that still remains.

## Guardrails

- Never treat implied access as approved access.
- Stop and escalate when scope interpretation becomes ambiguous.
- Keep all later testing traceable to scoping decisions.

## Evidence

- Scope documents, approvals, account lists, and communication records.
- Notes showing why each test path was considered in-scope.
