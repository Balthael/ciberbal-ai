# Workflow Pack — Enumeration

## Objective

Guide enumeration toward concrete attackable paths by turning broad recon into validated service, identity, and permission knowledge.

## Checklist

- Map reachable services, exposed functions, permissions, identities, and state transitions.
- Confirm what is accessible versus what is merely visible.
- Convert discovered surface into ranked validation opportunities.

## Outputs

- Enumerated attack surface with trust-boundary context.
- Ranked list of candidate exploit or abuse paths.
- Notes on dead ends and disproven assumptions.

## Guardrails

- Do not confuse banner/version trivia with actionable exposure.
- Keep enumeration safe, repeatable, and inside approved limits.
- Prefer evidence-backed prioritization over curiosity-driven sprawl.

## Evidence

- Service inventory, permission matrices, endpoint lists, and identity notes.
- Command/output logs showing what was actually reachable.
